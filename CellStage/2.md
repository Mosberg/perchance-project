Overview
- Goal: Implement a Cell Stage-like minigame where the player controls a growing cell in a top-down primordial ocean, collecting food, parts, and DNA to evolve into a new stage. Core loop: explore → eat → grow → buy parts → unlock evolution. [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)
- Tech: Phaser 4 (Arcade or Matter physics — Arcade for performance/simple collisions, Matter for richer physics). Example Phaser physics guidance referenced. [phaser](https://phaser.io/tutorials/box2d-tutorials/top-down-car-physics)

Design Summary
- Play area: a bounded scrolling ocean scene with parallax background. Spawn dynamic items: food (plant particles, meat chunks), NPC cells (various sizes/AI), meteor shards (part pickups), environmental obstacles (rocks, currents, bubbles). [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)
- Progression: discrete growth tiers (5 major tiers with sublevels). Player grows by accumulating DNA from food; reaching max progress triggers "brain" and evolution cutscene. [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)
- Difficulty modes change NPC aggression, spawn rates, and damage values (Easy / Normal / Hard). [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

Gameplay Systems (high level)
1. Movement
- Use physics body with velocity and drag (Arcade) or force/impulse (Matter). Provide three movement part types:
  - Flagella: steady speed, low acceleration.
  - Jet: short high-speed bursts.
  - Cilia: increases angular/turning rate.
- Implementation note: map input (WASD or pointer) to desired velocity; apply jet as impulse with cooldown. See Phaser physics examples for velocity handling. [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55DzXMkCfVA)

2. Feeding & Diet system
- Food categories: Plant, Meat, Egg.
- Mouth types (player state):
  - Filter Mouth (plants), Jaw (meat & living cells), Proboscis (drain living cells & plants but cannot directly eat dead meat).
- Eating rules:
  - On overlap with edible object, consume and grant DNA and HP if applicable.
  - If object is a dead cell (meat chunks), require Jaw to pick up.
  - Proboscis drains living cell health over time rather than instant meat chunks.
- Dietary tracking: maintain counters (plantEaten, meatEaten) to compute end-of-stage Trait Card (Herbivore/Omnivore/Carnivore). [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

3. Growth & Progression
- Player has growthProgress (0..100). Each food unit → +DNA and +growthProgress proportional to food value. Reaching thresholds triggers visual scale increases and unlocks higher-level NPC spawns.
- Use 5 main thresholds (e.g., 0–20, 20–40, 40–60, 60–80, 80–100) for major tiers; within tiers spawn sublevel content. [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

4. Parts / DNA economy
- DNA is currency; each food unit yields ~1 DNA (cap optional). Parts cost DNA; parts unlocked by meteor pickups or killing cells with that part.
- Parts: mouths, spikes, movement (flagella/jet/cilia), poison, electric, eyes.
- Parts stacking rules: most parts stack to increase effectiveness; some (Cilia, eyes) are non-stackable in effect (only influence once). [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

5. Combat & Damage
- Damage model: objects have hp. Size categories affect damage multiplier:
  - Prey: small, 1 HP
  - Peer: medium, higher HP
  - Predator: large
  - Epic: massive, one-hit danger unless countered
- Player attacks: Jaw (bite instant damage), Spike (ram damage), Poison (trail), Electric (periodic shock area). Immunities granted if player has same part (poison/electric). [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

6. Environment & Hazards
- Currents: apply persistent velocity vector in regions (tile or circular areas). Speed multiplier when aligned with current.
- Obstacles: static bodies that block movement and can be used defensively.
- Meteor shards: spawn randomly; colliding grants new part unlocks.
- Eggs, bubbles, crystals with defined interactions. [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

Data Models (JSON-like)

- PlayerCell
  - id, position, velocity, scale, hp, dna, growthProgress
  - parts: { mouths: [], spikes:[], movement:[], utilities:[] }
  - dietCounters: {plant: int, meat: int, eggs:int}
  - immunities: {poison:bool, electric:bool}

- FoodItem
  - id, type (Plant/Meat/Egg), valueDNA, hpRestore, position, size

- NPCCell
  - id, aiType (passive/skittish/hostile), sizeCategory, parts[], hp, speed, lootParts[]

- PartPickup
  - id, partType, rarity, grantedPart

- EnvironmentRegion
  - id, type (current, poisonCloud, sparkField), effectParameters

Spawn & Balancing Parameters (example)
- baseFoodSpawnRate = 1 per 3s per screen-area
- npcSpawnDistribution per growth tier (increase predator probability each tier)
- dnaPerPlant = 1, dnaPerMeatChunk = 1–3 depending on size
- progressPerDNA = 100 / goalDNA (goalDNA ≈ 65 as reference)

AI & Behavior
- Simple state machine:
  - Idle (wander with Perlin/random), Flee (if threatened), Chase (if carnivore & target smaller), Attack (on contact), Eat (consume food).
- Aggression scales with difficulty and NPC traits. Epic NPCs: large radius, rare spawn, specific weaknesses that can be scripted (e.g., stunned by Electric, vulnerable to Spike on tail). [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

Collision & Physics choices
- Arcade Physics: simple AABB/circle collisions, predictable performance, easy overlap handling — recommended for large numbers of particles and simple movement. [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55DzXMkCfVA)
- Matter Physics: realistic forces, collisions, rotation — choose if you want rich interactions like exact bouncing from spikes or physics-based current flow. [phaser](https://phaser.io/tutorials/box2d-tutorials/top-down-car-physics)

UI & Feedback
- Bottom progress bar with vertical markers for growth tiers.
- HUD: DNA count, hp, current parts overview, diet meter (plant vs meat ratio).
- Visual cues: transparency -> opacity as growth, sparks for electric, green clouds for poison.
- Cutscenes: simple camera pans/zoom + sprite-scale animation for brain evolution.

Sample Code Sketches (Phaser-4 style pseudocode)
- Movement update (Arcade):
  - compute desiredVelocity from input and parts (jets add burst, cilia adjust angularSpeed).
  - player.body.setVelocity(desiredX, desiredY)
  - apply drag or damping for fluid feel. [youtube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=55DzXMkCfVA)

- Food overlap handler:
  - onOverlap(player, food) {
      if (food.type == 'meat' && !player.hasPart('Jaw')) return;
      consume(food);
      player.dna += food.valueDNA;
      player.growthProgress += food.growthValue;
    } [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

- NPC AI simple:
  - if (distanceToPlayer < chaseRange && npc.aiType == 'hostile' && npc.size >= player.size) chasePlayer();
  - else wander();

Weakness & Epic mechanics
- Implement epic vulnerabilities as scripted triggers (e.g., if epic.type == 'Puffish' and within electricCooldown -> apply continuous damage). Use specific drop conditions for rarer parts via kill-credit logic. [spore.fandom](https://spore.fandom.com/wiki/Cell_Stage)

Balancing tips
- Cap DNA gain per food to avoid runaway growth; tune spawn rates per tier and difficulty; test with telemetry: average time-to-evolve target ≈ 7–12 minutes depending on difficulty.
- Use object pooling for frequent spawn/despawn (meat chunks, small NPCs) for performance.

Art & Audio
- Use silhouette/transparent sprites in early tiers transitioning to opaque; particle systems for poison/electric; water ripple shader for currents. Cutscenes can be made with camera-tweened sprite sequence.

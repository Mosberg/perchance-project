# Cell Stage

The Cell Stage is the opening phase of *Spore*, where the player controls a microscopic organism evolving in a primordial ocean. This stage introduces the core gameplay loop: **eat, grow, adapt, and evolve**.

## Overview

You begin as a single cell emerging from a meteor fragment in a fluid, top-down environment filled with:

- Other cells (prey, neutral, and predators)
- Plant matter and meat chunks
- Environmental objects (bubbles, rocks, currents)
- Meteor shards containing new parts

The primary goal is to **collect DNA by consuming food**, grow in size, and eventually **develop a brain**, which unlocks the transition to the Creature Stage.

Typical duration: ~5–15 minutes depending on difficulty.

### Core Mechanics

- **Feeding**: Consume plants, meat, or both to gain DNA.
- **Evolution**: Spend DNA to add parts in the Cell Editor.
- **Growth**: Your cell increases in size as you progress.
- **Interaction**: Avoid predators, hunt prey, and adapt to threats.

## Diet System

Your diet influences both gameplay and future progression:

- **Herbivore**: Eats plant matter (Filter Mouth).
- **Carnivore**: Eats meat and other cells (Jaw).
- **Omnivore**: Eats both (Proboscis or mixed mouths).

Diet is flexible during the stage but determines your **Trait Card** at completion, which affects abilities in later stages.

Example: If you mostly eat plants but occasionally attack enemies, you can still end as a herbivore.

## Progression

Progress is divided into roughly **5 major tiers** (with sub-levels), each increasing difficulty and introducing new parts and enemies.

### Early Stage (Levels 1–2)

- Learn basic movement and feeding.
- First encounters with passive and hostile cells.
- Unlock early parts:
  - Spike (offense)
  - Jet (speed)
  - Poison (area damage)

### Mid Stage (Levels 3–4)

- Introduction of advanced abilities:
  - Cilia (turning)
  - Proboscis (omnivore feeding)
  - Electric (area shock)
- Cells become more aggressive and diverse.
- Your cell becomes fully opaque (visual maturity indicator).

### Late Stage (Level 5)

- Larger, more complex ecosystems.
- Introduction of:
  - Egg-laying cells (Maa/Paa)
  - Swarming enemies (Juniors)
- Fewer oversized background creatures; more direct threats.

## Cell Types and Threat Levels

Cells are categorized by size and danger:

- **Prey**: Very small, die in one hit, minimal threat.
- **Peer**: Similar size, moderate risk.
- **Predator**: Larger, can consume smaller cells instantly.
- **Epic**: Massive, rare, extremely dangerous.

Example: A predator can instantly consume prey, while an Epic can eliminate the player in a single hit unless countered with specific abilities.

## Parts and Abilities

Parts are unlocked via meteor shards or defeating cells with new abilities. All parts cost DNA.

### Mouths

- **Jaw**: Carnivore; damages enemies and consumes meat.
- **Filter Mouth**: Herbivore; consumes plant matter.
- **Proboscis**: Omnivore; drains living cells and eats plants.

### Movement

- **Flagella**: Smooth, steady movement.
- **Jet**: Fast bursts of speed.
- **Cilia**: Improved turning control.

### Weapons

- **Spike**: Direct contact damage; strong early-game tool.
- **Poison**: Leaves damaging trails; grants immunity to poison.
- **Electric**: Area shock; effective against groups and Epics.

Note: Most parts can be stacked to increase effectiveness, except for certain abilities like turning (Cilia).

## Environment and Resources

Key objects encountered in the Cell Stage:

- **Plant Matter**: Primary food source for herbivores.
- **Meat Chunks**: Dropped from defeated cells; carnivore food.
- **Eggs**: Found late-stage; hatch into enemies but can be consumed.
- **Meteor Shards**: Unlock new parts.
- **Currents**: Affect movement speed and control.
- **Obstacles (rocks, shells)**: Used for defense and positioning.
- **Hazards**:
  - Poison clouds
  - Electric fields

Mastering movement through currents and around obstacles is essential for survival.

## Missions

Optional objectives provide bonus DNA:

- **Eat Food**: Consume a set number of food pieces.
- **Collect Parts**: Unlock all available abilities.

Completing all part unlocks grants full access to Cell Stage customization.

## Evolution and Transition

Once the progress bar is filled:

1. Your cell develops a **brain**.
2. A cutscene plays showing evolution toward land.
3. You receive a **Trait Card** based on diet:
   - Herbivore
   - Carnivore
   - Omnivore

This choice affects abilities and progression in all future stages.

## Strategy Guidelines

### General

- Balance **mobility, offense, and defense**.
- Use obstacles and currents to escape threats.
- Prioritize early survivability over optimization.

### Build Examples

- **Speed Build**: Jet + Cilia → strong evasion and control.
- **Offense Build**: Spike + Jaw → aggressive hunting.
- **Defense Build**: Poison + Electric → area denial and immunity.

### Diet Paths

- **Herbivore**: Focus on speed and avoidance.
- **Carnivore**: Invest early in spikes and mobility.
- **Omnivore**: Combine both strategies; most flexible.

Example: A Jet + Cilia + Proboscis build enables fast movement, sharp turning, and access to both plant and prey-based food sources.

## Difficulty Differences

- **Easy**: Fewer predators, slower enemies, no Epic cells.
- **Normal**: Balanced gameplay, Epics introduced.
- **Hard**: Aggressive AI, faster threats, early predators, more frequent Epics.

Difficulty significantly impacts enemy behavior, spawn rates, and survivability.
